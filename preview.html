Field Report Details

Location
📍 Grand Teton National Park, Wyoming
Date
📅 September 15, 2025
Weather
🌤️ Sunny, 65°F, light winds
Distance
🥾 18.5 miles
Duration
⏱️ 2 days, 1 night

The approach to Paintbrush Canyon offers some of the most dramatic alpine scenery in the lower 48 states.

Starting from the String Lake trailhead, we ascended through dense lodgepole pine forests, gradually gaining elevation as the trail opened to reveal sweeping views of Jackson Hole valley below. The vintage Kodachrome aesthetic of these mountains—with their layered sedimentary bands and weathered granite faces—evokes the classic National Park photography of the mid-20th century.

"In every walk with nature, one receives far more than he seeks." — John Muir

The overnight camp at Lake Solitude provided an opportunity to document the transition from day to dusk, capturing the warm golden hour light as it painted the Teton peaks in shades of amber and crimson—colors that perfectly match our vintage documentary theme.
